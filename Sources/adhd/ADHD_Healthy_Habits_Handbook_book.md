## Wellbeing Lesson #1: Who Are You? Understanding You and Your ADHD

Self-awareness is a fundamental aspect of wellbeing. Without it, you lack important information about your areas of strength and need, and how your ADHD symptoms (and symptoms of other potentially co-occurring conditions) uniquely show up in your life. As you build self-awareness, you’ll learn which aspects of your life require more attention so that you can honor your needs and thrive.
