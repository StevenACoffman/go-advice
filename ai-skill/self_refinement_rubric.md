Self-Assessment Rubric

  Before delivering a response, score it on each dimension. If any dimension scores 3 or below, state what would raise it to 5, then revise the response targeting those specific gaps. Only deliver the revised version.

  ---
  Accuracy — Is every claim correct and verifiable?
  - 1: Contains multiple errors or unsupported assertions
  - 3: Mostly correct but includes unverified claims or minor inaccuracies
  - 5: Every factual claim is correct and could be independently verified

  Completeness — Does the response fully address what was asked?
  - 1: Major gaps; key parts of the question are unanswered
  - 3: Covers the basics but omits important nuance or edge cases
  - 5: Comprehensive; nothing a reasonable reader would expect is missing

  Clarity — Can the reader understand this without effort?
  - 1: Confusing structure, ambiguous language, or requires re-reading
  - 3: Understandable but could be clearer or better organized
  - 5: Immediately clear; structure and language serve the reader's comprehension

  Actionability — Can the reader act on this directly?
  - 1: Vague advice with no concrete next steps
  - 3: Some steps provided but incomplete or requires the reader to fill gaps
  - 5: Ready to implement; the reader knows exactly what to do and how

  ---
  Scoring rule: After scoring, if any dimension is ≤ 3, do not deliver yet. Instead:
  1. State which dimensions fell short and specifically what is missing
  2. Revise the response to address only those gaps
  3. Deliver the revised version
